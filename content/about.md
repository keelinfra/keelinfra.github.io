keelinfra builds production-grade, self-hosted distributions of open-source infrastructure.
The first is [a Keycloak distribution](/keycloak/) — HA, backups, monitoring, and upgrade paths
re-proven nightly in public CI.

## Deliberately small

keelinfra is not a platform company with an account team. It's engineers — EU-based — who wrote
every playbook in the distribution and publish every claim with a receipt. When you email
[hello@keelinfra.io](mailto:hello@keelinfra.io), the person who answers is the person who will do
the work. For the buyer we serve, that's not a limitation; it's the point:

- **You talk to the engineer, not an account manager.**
- **Everything is in the open** — the [code](https://github.com/keelinfra/keycloak), the
  [docs](/docs/keycloak/), the [CI runs](https://github.com/keelinfra/keycloak/actions), the
  [claims ledger](/evidence/). A small company can't hide behind process, so we don't try.
- **We say "you don't need us" when you don't.** The distribution is complete without a
  subscription or a service engagement — that's by design.

## The operating principles

1. **Proven, not promised.** No claim ships without a public CI run, a published measurement, or
   a drill you can run yourself.
2. **The ops knowledge is the product** — and it's Apache-2.0. No open-core split, no gated
   features, no relicense rug-pull.
3. **Your machines, your data.** Nothing phones home. There is nothing of yours for us to lose.
