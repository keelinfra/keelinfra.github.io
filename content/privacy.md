This page is about the **keelinfra.io website**. The Keycloak distribution sends nothing to us
(see [Security](/security/)).

**Analytics.** We use Google Analytics to see which pages are read and how people find them:
pages viewed, links clicked, referrer, browser and device, and approximate location. It sets
cookies (`_ga`) and keeps the data for 14 months. No advertising features, and it is never
linked to your name or email.

**Opt out** by blocking cookies for this site, using a content blocker, or installing Google's
[opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). The site works the same.

**Hosting and email.** The site is served by GitHub Pages. Mail to
[hello@keelinfra.io](mailto:hello@keelinfra.io) is forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing and used
only to reply to you.

**Questions or deletion requests:** [hello@keelinfra.io](mailto:hello@keelinfra.io).
