This page covers the **keelinfra.io website** only. The Keycloak distribution makes no network
calls to keelinfra: no telemetry, no license checks, no usage reporting (see
[Security](/security/)).

## Analytics

The website uses **Google Analytics 4** to understand which pages are read and how visitors find
them. It records:

- pages viewed, how far you scroll, and time on page
- links you follow to other sites (for example GitHub), and clicks on our email links, including
  the subject line the link pre-fills (such as "Migration / upgrade"), not anything you write
- the referring site or search engine, your browser, device type and operating system
- approximate location (country and city), which Google derives from your IP address; Google
  Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses

Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (`_ga` and `_ga_<ID>`) to tell one visit from a
returning one. They last up to two years. We do not use advertising features, remarketing or
Google signals, and we do not combine this data with anything else. Event-level data is kept for
14 months, after which only aggregated reports remain.

**Opting out:** block cookies for keelinfra.io in your browser, use a content blocker, or install
Google's [opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). The site works the
same either way.

## Hosting

The site is static and served by GitHub Pages. GitHub processes the requests it serves (including
your IP address), as any web host does; see
[GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Email

Mail to [hello@keelinfra.io](mailto:hello@keelinfra.io) is forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing to
a mailbox we read. We see your address and what you write, use it only to reply and to do the work
you asked about, and never add you to a list you did not ask to join.

## Your rights and contact

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email
[hello@keelinfra.io](mailto:hello@keelinfra.io). Analytics data is not tied to your name or email,
so we cannot look it up by person; the opt-out above stops it being collected.
