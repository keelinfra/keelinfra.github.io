FAQ

Straight answers

Do I need the subscription to run keelinfra?

No. The distribution is complete without it — install, upgrades, drills, monitoring, everything. The subscription adds offline bundles, CVE guidance and people.

Per node vs per user — the math

A 3-node HA cluster serving 50,000 users is $4,500/year. A managed tier at $599/month is $7,188/year before add-ons — and per-MAU vendors charge multiples of that. Growth costs you nothing here.

What does "fixed scope" mean?

We agree on the deliverables and the price before work starts. If it takes us longer, that's our problem, not your invoice.

What are your support hours?

EU business hours, response within one business day. Emergency help is best-effort — we do not sell a 24/7 SLA we cannot staff honestly.

Invoicing?

EU-based, invoices with VAT. Standard rails — no procurement portal required.
