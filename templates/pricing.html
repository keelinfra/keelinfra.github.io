FAQ

Straight answers

Do I need the subscription to run keelinfra?

No. The distribution is complete without it — install, upgrades, drills, monitoring, everything. The subscription adds CVE guidance, runbooks and people, and offline bundles once they ship.

Per cluster vs per user — the math

A 3-node HA cluster serving 50,000 users is $4,500/year. A managed tier at $599/month is $7,188/year before add-ons — and per-MAU vendors charge multiples of that. Growth costs you nothing here.

What does "fixed scope" mean?

We agree on the deliverables and the price before work starts. If it takes us longer, that's our problem, not your invoice.

What are your support hours?

EU business hours, by email. We usually reply within one business day. Enterprise aims for the next business day; neither tier carries a contractual response-time SLA, and emergency help is best-effort — we do not sell a 24/7 SLA we cannot staff honestly.

Invoicing?

Invoices on request, paid by bank transfer. No procurement portal required.
