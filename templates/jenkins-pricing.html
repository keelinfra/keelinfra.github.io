FAQ

Straight answers

Do I need to pay to use Agent8?

No. Everything in the repository is free, with no gated features. Paying adds checks on your platform, builds ready ahead of your upgrades, and people.

Could we build this ourselves?

Yes, and the code is open if you want to. The work is in keeping up: a new Jenkins release every week, a rising minimum agent version, and platforms CI cannot rent. For most teams, support costs less than an engineer's time spent on that.

What does a pilot not cover?

Plugins and job types outside the jobs we run with you. The report lists what was and was not exercised, so you know what is still unverified.

What are your support hours?

EU business hours. We do not sell a 24/7 SLA we cannot staff honestly.
